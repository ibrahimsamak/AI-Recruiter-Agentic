# Cover letter

## Rules
- Ground every claim in the candidate's real resume — never fabricate.
- Keep it to 3–4 short paragraphs (roughly 200–300 words).
- Address the specific company and role; no generic "To whom it may concern".
- Professional, warm, and direct — no clichés or filler ("hardworking team player").

## Structure
1. **Hook** — the role, and the single strongest reason the candidate fits it.
2. **Evidence** — 1–2 concrete accomplishments from the resume that map to the posting's top requirements.
3. **Fit / motivation** — why this company/team specifically; tie to something real about the role.
4. **Close** — a brief, confident call to action.

## Procedure
1. Reuse the top ~8 requirements already extracted during tailoring.
2. Pick the 2 accomplishments with the strongest, most quantifiable evidence.
3. Draft, then verify every factual claim traces back to the source resume before returning.
